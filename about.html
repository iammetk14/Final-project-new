About Codingal:

Codingal is a place where children, not only learn coding, but also a mindset that can never be developed normally. They learn how to create websites, Games and other things that they usually use and love online. This not only makes them happy, but developes a skill which is unmatchable. Codingal invites all children to join us on a journey to make the youth, future, country and resources even better. Coding here is just a big part of the huge puzzle the chilldren have to create. All management and teachers help us greatly and care about the students!
